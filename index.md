# Muxin Wei's Homepage
## About Me

My name is Muxin Wei (魏木鑫), currently a final year M.Sc. student in Biomedical Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology supervised by [Shuqing Chen](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=ZSBEUzLyx7IC&hl=en). Meanwhile, it is an honour to collaborate with [Dr. Zhicheng Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=82GMBFcAAAAJ&hl=en) on medical image analysis. 

## Research Interests
My research interests primarily lie in biomedical imaging, with a particular focus on image analysis, and its application in decision support in conjunction with clinical reports. The goal is to develop trustworthy and interpretable AI models that improve accuracy and efficiency for real-world healthcare tasks.
